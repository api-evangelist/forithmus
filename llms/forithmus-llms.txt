# Forithmus

> Forithmus builds foundation models for medical imaging (Zurich, Switzerland; pre-seed backed by Point Nine and e2vc). Its developer-facing product is the Forithmus Research Hub — a Challenge Platform where teams host and enter medical-imaging benchmarks by submitting Docker containers or prediction files, with leaderboards, credits/compute tiers, collections, forums, and groups. The public REST API lives at https://research.forithmus.com/api and is described by an OpenAPI 3.1.0 spec (172 paths, 213 operations, 24 tags). This llms.txt was generated by API Evangelist from the provider's spec and public surface.
